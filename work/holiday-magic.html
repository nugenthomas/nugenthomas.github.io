Etsy: Holiday 2018

Brand & Motion Design
2018

Etsy's seller 2018 holiday campaign to prepare sellers for the biggest shopping frenzy of the year with tips, tools, and reminders.

Etsy's seller 2018 holiday campaign involves various channels of communication. Our goal was to prepare seller for the biggest shopping frenzy of the year. Equipping them with tips, tools, reminders to help ensure the best customer experience for buyers. On top of motivating the seller we also ended the year by rewarding them with "feel-good" sentiments, through their personalized year in review page.
