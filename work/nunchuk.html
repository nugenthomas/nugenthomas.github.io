Nunchuk: Collaborative Bitcoin Custody

Product Design
2022

Multisig keeps Bitcoin safe, but coordinating it across a team is messy. As the founding designer, I partnered with the CEO to take the product from zero to launch, bringing multisig security and team coordination into one workflow. Launched at Bitcoin 2022 with first place, 10K+ downloads in the first week, and adopted by the Freedom Convoy organizers to manage and distribute Bitcoin donations.

Multisig is secure. Using it as a team is messy.

Multisig wallets need multiple people to sign before a transaction goes through. The security model is strong. The way teams actually run it is not. Most teams coordinate keys, approvals, and decisions in chat threads, email, and spreadsheets.

For an organization holding real money, that fragmentation has real costs. Missed signatures delay transactions. Misaligned policies create risk. New team members get onboarded into a workflow scattered across half a dozen tools.

Teams already coordinated. Just not in the product.

Talking to teams running multisig, the friction was almost never the security model itself. They understood multisig. The friction was everything around it: who signs next, whether to approve, why a past decision was made. The opportunity was to bring those conversations into the wallet.

Make the wallet feel like a shared workspace

If multisig is how the team protects the funds, the product should be how the team works on the funds together. I designed the wallet less like a vault and more like a shared workspace. Multisig still does the protecting, but it stops being the only thing on screen.

The product

Three pieces brought the thesis to life: in-product coordination, collaborative wallet creation, and a shared transaction workflow.

In-product coordination

I brought conversation into the wallet itself, attached to the wallet, the transaction, or the signer involved. Approvals, context, and history live where the work happens, not in a side channel.

Collaborative wallet creation

Setting up a multisig wallet usually means trading extended public keys in private messages. I turned it into an invitation flow. One organizer starts the wallet, invites the other signers, and the product walks the group through setting policies and confirming keys together.

Shared transaction workflow

A transaction is created, reviewed, signed by the required quorum, and broadcast. Each screen shows where the transaction stands, who has acted, and what is needed next. No more chasing signers in chat.

Outcome

Collaborative custody hit 10K+ downloads in the first week after launch. We won first place in the Infrastructure category at Bitcoin 2022 Pitch Day.

The Freedom Convoy organizers chose Nunchuk to receive and distribute Bitcoin donations to truckers, all from inside the product. It was a real-world test of whether a team of non-experts could run shared custody under pressure, and it worked.
