Etsy: Seller Order Management

Product Design
2021

The app was missing order management features sellers had on web. I led the design for three: private notes, mark as shipped with tracking, and a repeat buyer signal. It seeded later improvements that cut case rates up to 9% and lifted repeat purchases by 3%.

30.7% of top and power sellers added private notes to an order

The data showed notes mattered, but not how often. With the order page already packed, the PM and I agreed to keep the placement quiet.

I used a dark cockpit pattern: notes only appear when a buyer or seller adds one. The entry point lives under the overflow menu.

Solving the biggest buyer pain point through the seller workflow

38.6% of sellers buy shipping labels outside Etsy, but the app did not let them mark those orders as shipped. The Buy on Etsy team told me this hurt buyers too. Not knowing when a package would arrive was their top pain point.

I designed the flow for both sides. We considered making tracking required, but 14.2% of sellers skip it on purpose for low-cost items like stickers, where tracking costs more than the item. We kept it optional and made its value obvious instead.

Sellers want to know who is a repeat buyer so they can reward loyalty

Web had a repeat buyer badge. The app did not. Sellers mostly use the app to message buyers and check orders, so the signal matters there.

On web, the badge only lived in the order tab. I extended it to the message tab so sellers can spot a repeat buyer in conversation, before the next purchase.

Thinking beyond the MVP

My hypothesis: more concrete detail on a returning buyer makes sellers more likely to go above and beyond. I explored a stronger version of the badge: a banner that shows loyalty by money spent, with a drill-down into past interactions. This hypothesis is to be tested in the near future.

Impact

The project made seller order management more context-rich and actionable. Sellers no longer had to track details across messages, mental notes, and disconnected tools.

Later improvements built on this foundation cut case rates up to 9%, lifted repeat purchases by 3%, and reduced escalation and refund costs. Private notes and the repeat buyer signal stayed in the product, with continued investment across research, analytics, QA, and roadmap work.
